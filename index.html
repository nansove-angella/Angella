UGANDA MARTYRS UNIVERSITY

Uganda Martyrs University is private University affiliated with the Roman Catholic Church in Uganda.It is a chartered university. and offers degrees,masters and PHDS because it was accepted and is licensed by Uganda National Council for Higher Education.

The University students are equipped with quality education and are successful in their careers.

The University Environment

The environment is conducive and also comprises beautiful green pastures and vegetation. It is also noise free and a place where it is good to be.

Steps for Student Registration

Inorder for any student at UMU to register they must have a university email

  1. Search "register.umu.ac.ug" on the chrome web browser
  2. Sign in your university account
  3. Choose your UMU email
  4. Enter your password and then register
